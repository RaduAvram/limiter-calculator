Detailed breakdown
How it works
  • What the number means. The PA2 threshold is a level at the output in dBFS, where 0 dBFS = +20 dBu (the PA2's maximum output) and −20 dBFS = 0 dBu. It is measured after the crossover and output gain, so it is an absolute output level.
  • How the limiter acts. PeakPlus uses RMS detection at a fixed ∞:1 ratio, so the sustained level never goes above the threshold. Transients are soft-clipped but can still overshoot by up to 3 dB.
  • dbx's own method. Set the threshold just below the level that clips the amp: amp clip in dBu minus 20. Lower it 3 dB more "to be absolutely safe" against overshoot. Example from the manual: amp clips at +6 dBu → −14 dBFS, or −17 dBFS with the cushion.
  • What this calculator adds. Three ceilings, and the lowest wins: (1) amp clip, minus the cushion; (2) the speaker's continuous rating at the sustained level; (3) the speaker's program rating during 3 dB overshoot transients. A common reduction is then applied if the venue limit needs it.
  • Amp input limit. Each amp's input stage clips at its maximum input level: T6.5 +12.2 dBu (10 dBV, Outline manual), L2400 +21 dBu. If that stage comes before the pot, turning the pot down raises the level needed for full power but not the input limit, so past a point the input clips before the amp reaches full power. The calculator treats this as a fourth ceiling. The amp's CLIP LED may only show output clipping, not input clipping.
  • Amp pot attenuation raises the PA2 level needed for full power, which raises all three ceilings by the same amount. A 0–10 knob is converted with an assumed audio taper (knob 10 = 0 dB, 5 ≈ −20 dB, 0 = muted), which is only an estimate. To measure it: disconnect the speakers, feed the amp a steady sine tone (around 60 Hz for the sub) and read the output with a multimeter on AC volts, first at knob 10, then at your setting. Attenuation = 20 × log10(V10 ÷ Vsetting). Example: 40 V at 10 and 5 V at your setting is 18 dB.
  • Program crest factor doesn't enter the threshold. The RMS detector holds the sustained level, and the soft clipper handles peaks. Sustained power is shown as a worst case (sine or heavily compressed program). Normal music averages less.
  • Final check (manual p. 21). Disconnect the speakers, play pink noise, raise each amp until its clip LED just lights, then lower the PA2 threshold until it stops. Use the calculator's value as the starting point and the clip LEDs as the final word.
  • The venue level assumes free-field falloff (6 dB per doubling). Indoors it will be higher, so measure and adjust the dBA correction.
  • Mono summing of L+R into one bass feed isn't modelled. If the sum adds level, lower the bass threshold to match.
Notes and sources
  • Olive cabinet: Beyma 10CMV2 woofer, 8 Ω, 250 W AES / 500 W program, 95 dB 1 W/1 m. Beyma SMC-8060 tweeter, 8 Ω, 107 dB 1 W/1 m, reduced by a 12 dB pad, which brings it close to the woofer's level. The cabinet uses 95 dB. The SMC-8060 without /N is taken to have the same power rating as the /N.
  • Olive power is the sum of the two drivers' ratings: 300 W continuous (250 + 50), 600 W peak (500 + 100).
  • Large top: JBL 2226H woofer (97 dB) plus Beyma CP650Ti (110 dB). The sensitivity uses the JBL figure only, so the real cabinet level is probably higher.
  • Bass: Dynacord SRC 400 LO, 800 W rated, 1600 W program, 4 Ω, 100 dB. The 1600 W is a program rating, so the peak check is conservative.
  • Bass amp: Outline T6.5, 950 W / 1700 W / 3000 W at 8 / 4 / 2 Ω, 38.2 dB gain, 1.12 V sensitivity. Full-range amp: Dynacord L2400, 2 × 750 / 1200 / 1500 W at 8 / 4 / 2 Ω, 26 dB constant-gain mode.
  • PDF reference values (pots at 0 dB): sub −0.9 dBu and top +13.0 dBu, set at the speakers' continuous ratings. The T6.5 at −18 dB gives +17.1 dBu. With the 3 dB cushion the top becomes +11.0 dBu, because the L2400 clips at about +14.0 dBu, and +13.0 dBu plus 3 dB of overshoot would clip it by about 2 dB.
  • Outline T2.5/T4.5/T6.5 manual (outline.it, 2024): level knobs marked 0–10 with no dB scale; 950/1700/3000 W = 87.2/82.5/77.5 V rms at 8/4/2 Ω; gain 38.2 dB; sensitivity 1.12 V; max input 10 dBV.
  • DriveRack PA2 manual (5044138-B): limiter pp. 43–44, gain structure and limiter calibration pp. 19–21, output meters p. 4.
